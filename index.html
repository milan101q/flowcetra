FAQ

Questions, answered

What is FlowCetra?
FlowCetra builds custom operational web apps on top of a Google Sheet you already own. You get a clean, mobile-friendly app and automation on top, while your data stays in a sheet you control.
Do FlowCetra web apps require a monthly subscription?
No. FlowCetra uses an ownership model with no monthly subscription and no hidden fees — unlike traditional SaaS that charges recurring per-user fees and renewal costs.
How long does it take to launch a FlowCetra platform?
Most platforms go live in days, not months. FlowCetra analyzes your workflow, configures the platform around your data, and launches a ready-to-use portal for your team quickly.
Who owns the data in a FlowCetra app?
You do. Your data stays in a Google Sheet you own and control, with the FlowCetra web app and automation layered on top of it.
What types of businesses is FlowCetra built for?
FlowCetra serves operational businesses across 12+ industries, including auto dealerships, repair shops, contractors, towing companies, inspection companies, logistics dispatch, roofing, cleaning, property management, warehouses, moving companies, and insurance adjusters.
How much does CarConnect cost?
CarConnect is a flat $199 per month. It posts your entire vehicle inventory to Facebook Marketplace automatically twice a month, and 20% of your plan goes toward boosting your listings.
Can FlowCetra apps be used on mobile?
Yes. Every FlowCetra platform is mobile-friendly out of the box and works on any mobile device with no app store download required.

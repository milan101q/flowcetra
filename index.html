MyCarInspector.comNEW!

A nationwide mobile pre-purchase car inspection marketplace.

Car buyers book an inspection online, a vetted local inspector is dispatched to the vehicle, and the buyer gets a full report with photos. We designed it, built every line of it, and we operate it.

  • Online booking with instant VIN decoding
  • Automatic dispatch to a network of 200+ vetted inspectors
  • Inspector app for checklists, photos and written findings, right from a phone
  • Professional PDF reports and secure online payment through Stripe
  • Admin back office for jobs, inspectors, payouts and alerts
Built & operated by Web App Solutions LLC

CarConnect.click

A platform that posts dealership and auction vehicles on Facebook Marketplace.

CarConnect takes the vehicles of car dealerships and auto auctions and posts them on Facebook Marketplace, where buyers message the seller directly. It runs on a posting platform we wrote ourselves.

  • Posts vehicles from car dealerships and auto auctions on Facebook Marketplace
  • Puts every vehicle in front of Marketplace’s local car buyers
  • Buyers contact the dealership or auction directly through Marketplace messages
Built & operated by Web App Solutions LLC
Visit CarConnect.click »
#3REPORTS

PPIPro.click

A platform for generating pre-purchase inspection reports.

PPIPro walks an inspector through the vehicle on a phone and turns what they find into a professional, client-ready pre-purchase inspection report the moment the inspection ends.

  • Guided inspection checklist built for technicians in the field
  • Photo capture attached right to the findings
  • Instant client-ready PDF report, no retyping back at the office
Built by Web App Solutions LLC
Visit PPIPro.click »

  • Custom-designed menu pages built around the kitchen’s own photography and brand
  • One-tap ordering deep-linked into the existing Square Online store
